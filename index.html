Hold Z or Shift to press the clutch, X to let it out quickly, C to let it out slowly. W/S set the gas (it stays where you leave it). R starts the engine (clutch in if you’re in gear). N 1 2 3 shift — clutch in first.

Find the bite point

A first-gear game — not a driving lesson, and not for use while driving.

  1. Press the clutch all the way down (left pedal or Z) and hit Start engine.
  2. Add a little gas so the tach sits in the green band, around 2,000 rpm.
  3. Let the clutch up slowly. When the marker enters the gold friction zone, the engine takes the load — that’s the bite.
  4. Keep a little gas and finish letting the clutch out as you roll. Dumping it, or giving it no gas, will stall. Too much gas burns the clutch.

Optional stretch: once you’re rolling, clutch in and shift to 2nd, then 3rd.
