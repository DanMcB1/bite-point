# Bite Point

A free first-gear browser game about finding the clutch **friction zone** (the bite point). Ease the clutch out, add a little gas, and try for a smooth pull-away.

**This is a game, not a driving lesson.** It is not driver’s education and is not enough preparation to operate a real manual car. Do not use it while driving. Pedal feel on a screen is not the same as a real clutch.

No ads. No tracking or analytics. Nothing is uploaded; a local-only counter in your browser remembers how many attempts and clean launches you’ve made on this device.

Play it here: <https://danmcb1.github.io/bite-point/>

Or open `index.html` in a browser.

## Controls

**On a phone or with a mouse:** drag the large **Clutch** (left) and **Gas** (right) sliders. They stay where you leave them.

**Keyboard**

- `Z` or `Shift` — clutch down
- `X` — let the clutch out quickly (easy to dump)
- `C` — let the clutch out slowly (feather)
- `W` / `S` or arrow keys — more / less gas (the pedal holds its position)
- `R` or Space — start the engine (clutch in if you’re in gear)
- `N`, `1`, `2`, `3` — shift (clutch in first, or you’ll grind)

Optional stretch: once you’re rolling in first, clutch in and shift to 2nd, then 3rd.
